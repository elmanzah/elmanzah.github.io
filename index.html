What we do

We define the role before we fill it.

The usual way

Match keywords to a job description

A recruiter takes your posting and searches for people whose résumés echo it. If the description is off — wrong reporting line, wrong skill mix, wrong outcome — the search is wrong before it starts, and you find out three months in.

The Luxlen way

Define what the role must own — then source

We help you scope the reporting line, the outcome the role is accountable for, and the real blend of engineering, product, and customer skill behind the title. Only then do we go find the person who fits it.

The result: fewer mis-hires, shorter searches, and roles that still make sense a year from now.
